How to Play

- To begin, drag your Player Token chip to the middle location or a location of your choosing.

- A standard game will have the player start with 3 Resource Points (RP) assigned to their Life Support (Black D20).

- The Standard Player Ability is if both D6 are 1s, your Fuel Points becomes 12.

- CONTROLS: Drag the Player Tokens and Civilization Tokens, Click Event Icon to Roll D6 and Advance Turns, D20s Increase with Left Clicks and Decrease with Right Clicks or use the scroll wheel up and down (Left Click each D6 individually to manually reroll if needed).

- Use the buttons below the trackers to select boards, roll a new board, or reset the game.

- Click the Event Icon image on the right to initiate a new turn or start the game.

- Your D6 Fuel Points (FP) value is your available Fuel for this turn.

- Each turn, resolve the Event's impact: Resource Points must be managed by the Player, Most D6 calculations are done automatically (Event Modifier).

- The Player can sometimes become "hindered" from events, Player token becomes translucent in this state.

- To move, your Fuel Points must meet or exceed the crossing edge value of the TARGET location you intend to move onto.

- If your Fuel Points roll is succesful, then drag the Player Token chip to the new location.

- When you cross onto the target location you gain the + value (Blue) points into your Resource Points. You choose if it goes to Life Support (Black D20) or your Cargo (White D20)

* Must go to three other locations before you can recollect Resources from a location.

* Negative Event impacts effect Life Support RP always unless otherwise stated in the event description.

* The player must manually monitor Resource Points and account for effects from Events.

- If you are unable to make a move to a new location, you are stranded, then you must lose the - value (Orange) and re-roll a new turn.

- Losing all Life Support RP (Black D20) results in a game over!

- Manage how you store your collected Resources wisely.

- Resources can only be assigned when first collected, Cargo cannot be used for Life Support Resources after it has been stored.

- Planet locations may have Civilizations marked with Yellow Star Icons, up to 3 levels, the goal is to claim your own uncharted world.

- Use 10 Cargo Resource Points to deposit a "C" Civilization Token chip on a planet location that DOES NOT already have a Civilization.

* When you recollect resources from a world you have a civilization on, you gain +1 Resource Point for each token.

- Gather more Cargo Resources to upgrade a single Planet's Civilization to Level 3 using the token chips.

- Colonize and upgrade 2 uncharted worlds to level 3 in solo play to win the game!

- Explore and build up your frontier worlds!

- - Advanced Player Ability: Move diagonally by averaging the two adjacent values on the target card.

- - Balance how you divide your Resource Points and plan your journey so you don't get stranded!

- - Two players can play by racing to build their outposts: use shared Fuel Points, track RP and Cargo physically and separate, Build your own civilizations- first to Lvl 3 wins!
